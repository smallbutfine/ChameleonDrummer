Groove MIDI Dataset excerpts

This directory contains transformed drum-performance phrases derived from the
Groove MIDI Dataset (GMD) v1.0.0, made available by Google LLC under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license:

  Dataset: https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Source archive: groove-v1.0.0-midionly.zip
  Published source archive SHA-256:
  651cbc524ffb891be1a3e46d89dc82a1cecb09a57c748c7b45b844c4841dcc1e

The MIDI performances were recorded from drummers performing to a click in
multiple styles. This project extracts bar-bounded phrases, converts event
positions to 960 ticks per quarter note, maps source pitches to the project's
drum-instrument names at load time, and may further humanize or transform
phrases during song generation. Source performance IDs, style, meter, tempo,
performer IDs, and split are retained in gmd_patterns.json.
